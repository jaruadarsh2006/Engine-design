# Engine-design
Designed a detailed 4-stroke internal combustion engine in SolidWorks, demonstrating expertise in 3D part modeling, assembly design, and parametric modeling.
Performed motion analysis at 25fps to evaluate the complete intake, compression, power, and exhaust cycles, analyzing piston-crankshaft dynamics, valve timing, and component interactions under realistic operating conditions, enabling accurate assessment and optimization of engine performance and functionality.
